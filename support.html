Common questions

My progress disappeared after reinstalling.

Saves live only on your device, so deleting the app deletes your progress and reinstalling starts fresh. Cloud saves aren’t supported yet — they’re on the list.

Is there anything to buy?

No. There are no in-app purchases and no ads. Every character, building and upgrade is earned by playing.

I lost money after a failed commute.

That’s the game working as intended — wipe out on the way in and you lose the cash you were carrying. Get to the office.

Can I move my save to a new phone?

Not yet. Progress is tied to the device until cloud saves ship.

The game runs hot or drops frames.

Tell me your device model and roughly what was on screen. Performance reports with a device name are genuinely the most useful thing you can send.
