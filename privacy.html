Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 August 2026

Rat Race collects no data. None at all. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

Rat Race: Escape the 9-5 (“the Game”) is developed by Alexander Davis. This policy explains, in plain terms, what happens to information when you play.

What the Game stores

Your progress — in-game currency, buildings, roads, unlocked characters and your settings — is written to a single save file in the Game’s own storage area on your iPhone. That file never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or transmitted anywhere, and we have no way to read it.

What the Game does not do

  • No account creation, registration, or sign-in of any kind.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No personal information is collected, stored, sold, or shared — because none is ever gathered.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access is requested.
  • No in-app purchases. Everything in the Game is earned by playing it.

Deleting your data

Because everything lives on your device, deleting the app deletes all of it, immediately and permanently. There is no server-side copy to request or erase. Note that this also means progress cannot be recovered after a delete, and does not transfer to a new device.

Children

The Game collects no data from anyone, of any age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children because we do not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future update changes anything above — for example if in-app purchases or cloud saves are added — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the App Store release notes for that version.
